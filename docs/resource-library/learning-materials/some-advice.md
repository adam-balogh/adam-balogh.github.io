!!! abstract "Summary"

    Starting a career as a technical writer can be daunting, whether you're a recent graduate or transitioning from another field. This guide offers key insights and strategies to navigate the challenges and opportunities you may face.

    **Source: https://idratherbewriting.com/blog/career-advice-just-starting-out.html** by Tom Johnson on Aug 16, 2022

## The Experience Dilemma

Consider this scenario: you must choose a partner for a new project—one with 25 years of experience or one with 2 years. The seasoned writer might be resistant to change and overly reliant on outdated methods, while the novice might require extensive guidance and lack initiative. Ideally, you want to avoid becoming either extreme.

## Avoid Becoming "Jerry"

"Jerry" has 25 years of experience but is negative and resistant to change due to past frustrations with arbitrary deadlines, layoffs, and poor leadership. To avoid this trajectory, focus on:

1. **Reading**: Engage with high-value content to keep learning and maintain an open mind.
2. **Experimenting**: Try new tools, techniques, and processes to gain fresh insights.
3. **Asking Questions**: Foster curiosity and challenge your assumptions to discover new perspectives.
4. **Seeing Mental Models**: Understand different interpretations of the same data to stay flexible in your thinking.
5. **Taking Risks**: Embrace opportunities that push you out of your comfort zone to spur growth.

## Confronting Aging in Tech

Aging in the tech field can cause anxiety, but staying young at heart involves:

1. **Experimenting with new ideas**
2. **Changing perspectives**
3. **Reading constantly**
4. **Questioning existing knowledge**

## Avoid Becoming "Jayden"

"Jayden" is a novice who relies heavily on guidance and is reactive rather than proactive. To level up past novice mode:

1. **Be Proactive**: Regularly meet with product and engineering teams to identify and initiate documentation work.
2. **Develop Focus**: Minimize distractions and create periods of deep work to enhance productivity.
3. **Engage Reviewers**: Facilitate document reviews during meetings to ensure thorough feedback.
4. **Specialize Your Skills**: Develop expertise in areas like API documentation, developer UX, or content management to increase your value.
5. **Influence Through Writing**: Use your writing to communicate effectively and influence stakeholders within your organization.

## Conclusion

Avoiding the extremes of Jerry and Jayden means fostering curiosity, embracing change, and maintaining a proactive and engaged approach to your work. Whether you're just starting or are well into your career, these principles will help you become a valuable and respected technical writer.
